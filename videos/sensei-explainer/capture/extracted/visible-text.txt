Sensei — Capture, Map & Teach.
We want to position the software so that it compounds as knowledge base over time, captures information from experienced employees, filters out the business logic and is able to apply as well as teach new hires how to apply the knowledge in the company and what effects this has on business decisions.
Language agnostic (German/English, etc.) and unobtrusive. A greater purpose of knowledge consolidation through the company so that the precious knowledge does not get lost and can be transferred to others (humans and later AI agents).
App flow: Capture (expert works as usual in the ERP; a voice agent watches clicks and asks "why?" at natural pauses) → Debrief / Work Map (answers become rules, each linked to a screen moment and the expert's own words; expert confirms) → Teach (voice tutor watches a new hire on an unseen case, stops a wrong decision before save, explains it with the expert's reasoning and replays the expert's moment).
Demo: Sabine routes a €180 Claude invoice to the project lead; the rule is "limit €100 per project per month". Lena is stopped before approving a €90 invoice (€130 this month) and routes it correctly. Slogan: "Sensei, teach the next generation."
