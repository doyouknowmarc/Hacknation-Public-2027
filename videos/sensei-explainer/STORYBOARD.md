frame holds short, large copy that reveals in reading order. Look: Sensei app — paper/ink,

## Video direction

Calm, paper-and-ink. One idea per frame, revealed line by line at reading speed (~3 words/s).
Recurring device: a small indigo "Sensei orb" (circle + soft rings) that appears whenever Sensei acts.
Step chips "1 Capture · 2 Work Map · 3 Teach" recur in frames 4–7 so the architecture reads as one
pipeline. Shu red is reserved for the WHY and for stops/stamps. Blur-crossfades between frames.
