## Intent

A <60 s explainer of what Sensei does and why it matters, played at the Hack-Nation presentation.
Text-only (no voiceover) over a calm music bed. Concise and easy to understand.

Core purpose in the team's words: the software compounds as a knowledge base over time, captures
information from experienced employees, filters out the business logic, and can apply it as well as
teach new hires how to apply the knowledge and what effect this has on business decisions.
Language agnostic (German / English, etc.) and unobtrusive. Greater purpose: knowledge consolidation
across the company so precious knowledge is not lost and can be transferred to others — humans now,
AI agents later. The WHY is important.

## Customizations

- Show the architecture in plain terms: Capture → Work Map → Teach, feeding a growing knowledge base.
- Multilingual "Why?" (Warum? / Pourquoi? / ¿Por qué? / なぜ?).
- Music bed: generated with the team's own ElevenLabs Music API (HeyGen not signed in).
